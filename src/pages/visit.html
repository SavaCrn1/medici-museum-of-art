Getting here

The museum sits on East Market Street (OH‑82) in Warren.

{{map:SELF|Open in maps|btn}}

By car

From Cleveland, OH (approximately 68 miles): take I‑90 E and I‑80 E to OH‑11 S. Continue on OH‑11 S to exit 34 for OH‑82 W toward Warren. Merge onto OH‑82 W and take the East Market Street exit. Turn right onto East Market Street; the museum is on your right.

From Pittsburgh, PA (approximately 72 miles): take I‑376 W and merge onto I‑80 W toward Youngstown. Take exit 234B to merge onto OH‑11 N toward Warren. Take exit 34 for OH‑82 W toward Warren. Merge onto OH‑82 W and take the East Market Street exit. Turn right onto East Market Street; the museum is on your right.

Accessibility at the museum

If anything would make your visit difficult, contact us before you come and we will do what we can to help.

We are documenting the museum's step-free routes, seating and facilities so that visitors can plan ahead rather than ask. Until that is published here, please get in touch and we will describe the building honestly and arrange what you need.
