Support

Make a donation

Your generous support helps Medici Museum of Art keep general admission free.

Your gift helps the museum maintain free general admission, offer engaging public programs, present thought-provoking exhibitions, and provide community outreach. Please help us strengthen and enhance MMA for this and future generations. All donations are tax deductible.

Your support will fund Medici Museum of Art and its expansion throughout the country, and help cover the costs of bringing future exhibitions to life. Your gift positively affects our efforts to gather people in a meaningful way.

The Medici Museum is committed to providing our community and larger region access to exceptional art for free.
